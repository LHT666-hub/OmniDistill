# Source, rights, privacy, and identity

## Authority

Classify every source:

- user-owned;
- licensed for reuse;
- publicly accessible but copyrighted;
- confidential/internal;
- third-party personal data;
- unknown.

Public accessibility does not imply permission to redistribute. Generated Skills should contain derived, minimal rules rather than copied source text.

## Privacy

- Obtain authority before processing private messages, email, personnel records, or internal documents.
- Minimize collection to the intended task.
- Redact credentials, direct identifiers, private contact details, and irrelevant third-party data.
- Keep private source paths out of redistributable packages.
- Treat deleted or revoked material as unavailable in later versions.

## Identity

Default policy is `capability_only`. A generated Skill:

- must not claim to be the source person;
- must not assert access to private beliefs;
- must distinguish public evidence from inference;
- must state its temporal cutoff;
- must isolate optional persona style from capability.

## Licenses

Check each referenced repository before copying implementation or prose. Methodological inspiration is not permission to relicense source text. In particular, non-commercial share-alike material cannot be copied into an MIT package without satisfying its license.
