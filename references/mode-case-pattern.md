# Case-pattern mode

Sample both positive and negative cases. Record selection rules before extracting patterns.

For each case capture:

- initial conditions and objective;
- actors, resources, and constraints;
- intervention or structure;
- outcome and outcome definition;
- confounders and alternative explanations;
- missing data;
- whether the case was selected because it succeeded.

Label outputs as:

- `descriptive pattern`: commonly observed;
- `predictive heuristic`: useful for forecasting under stated conditions;
- `causal claim`: requires an appropriate research design and must not be inferred from frequency alone.

Do not turn fixed counts, scores, or templates into universal requirements without cross-context evidence.
