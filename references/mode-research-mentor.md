# Research-mentor mode

## Evidence signals

Extract per work:

- problem and claimed contribution;
- data or corpus;
- method and alternatives;
- evaluation or argument standard;
- baseline, comparison, robustness, or counterargument;
- limitation and stated uncertainty;
- authorship position and collaboration context;
- relation to earlier and later work.

## Attribution

Use:

- `individual` only for direct first-person statements, sole-authored work, repeated corresponding-author patterns, or corroborated decisions;
- `team` for stable lab practice;
- `coauthor` when contribution likely comes from a collaborator;
- `institution` for policy or infrastructure;
- `unknown` when the evidence cannot separate contributors.

Never convert “a coauthored paper used method M” into “the target always prefers M.”

## Cross-paradigm adaptation

Define method and evidence signals by research paradigm:

- experimental/computational: baselines, ablations, error analysis, reproducibility;
- empirical social science: construct validity, sampling, identification, robustness, interpretation;
- qualitative: case selection, reflexivity, coding, triangulation, negative cases;
- humanities: archive, textual evidence, conceptual precision, counter-reading;
- law: authority, doctrine, jurisdiction, case comparison, interpretive method;
- theory: assumptions, internal consistency, explanatory scope, counterexample.
